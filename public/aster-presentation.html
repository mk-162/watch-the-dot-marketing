01 · Overview

Two deliverables. One cohesive identity.

Aster's a live GPS tracking app for cycling events — the bit the sport calls "dot watching." The redesign elevates the app's visual language without touching business logic. The marketing site translates it into a public-facing story for organizers, athletes, and spectators.

Marketing site

A new home at watchthedot.cc

Nine static pages, sub-1-second loads, a hand-built animated "live dot" canvas in the hero. Built on Next.js + Tailwind. Ready to deploy to Vercel.

App redesign

Editorial polish, zero functional risk

Expanded design tokens, refreshed typography, new component primitives. The existing tracking, WebSocket, and API logic is fully intact.

13 · What's next

Ready when you are.

Both projects compile from a cold build. Suggested next moves, ordered by value over cost:

  1. Deploy marketing to watchthedot.cc — Vercel will pick up the Next.js 15 project as-is. Point DNS, ship.
  2. Wire the dark-mode toggle — the CSS variables are in place. A single classList.toggle('dark') flips the entire app.
  3. Connect CTAs to the real app — the "Get the app" and "Host an event" buttons point at placeholders; swap in your live URLs.
  4. Reduced-motion support — pause the hero canvas under prefers-reduced-motion.
  5. Replace the QR placeholder with a dynamic code that deep-links by user-agent.
  6. Surgical follow-ups on EventPage & Track — the bigger pages have 1,200+ lines of business logic; migrate remaining legacy styles to the new token scale incrementally, without risk to tracking behavior.
